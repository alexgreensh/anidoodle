# Rubber hose (1930s studio cartoon)

Module: `engine/src/canvas-core/rubberHose.ts` (+ `rubberHoseKit.ts`). Hero: a coffee pot mid-dance-step on a kitchen counter, steam curling.

**Medium, physically.** A blue-pencil rough on punched animation bond; traced in India ink at ONE pressure onto a clear cel; painted on the BACK of the cel in flat opaque greys; photographed and printed on black-and-white film.

**The marks.** A uniform ink line (no thick-thin) with a slight hand wobble, reseeded per drawing when animated (line boil on twos). Contours stop where a nearer shape starts: every part knocks its own interior out of the ink beneath it, and a union outline is stroked double then has its interior removed, so a glove of six overlapping pieces reads as one silhouette. Limbs are solid black noodles: one quadratic arc from shoulder to wrist, no elbow. White four-digit gloves, three stitch lines, flared cuff. Pie-cut pupils. Big oval shoes with one shine.

**Edge.** Hard and even. Paint never modelled; cast shadows are translucent flat shapes with no ink.

**Order.** Rough (line of action first, then masses, then parts) -> ink (character, then set) -> paint, flooded region by region from where the brush touched (character first) -> the pull-down: the drawing is threaded down through the projector gate and comes back as a print -> two seconds of dance on twos that lands on the key pose -> the hold (= the still).

**Palette.** Greys only: ink #16130f, paper whites, five greys; the print is toned warm (multiply #ecdfc6).

**Film.** Per-frame grain tile offset (soft overlay), lamp flicker, dust (dark = positive, light = negative), a hair, a sprocket scratch, a vignette from the lamp, the rounded gate. Gate weave (+/-1.7 px) only while projecting motion; the still uses seed 0 and no weave.

**Not the marker comic.** koi is modelled cel colour with a contour that thickens away from the light; this is one line weight, no modelling, greys, and a film print.

**Motion grammar.** On twos. Key poses on the beat (15 frames), the body leads and the limbs follow ~2 frames behind on the same arc, a hop of ~26 px between keys with stretch in the air and squash on landing. Loops land exactly on the key pose.
