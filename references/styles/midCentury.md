# Mid-century gouache (1950s-60s storybook)

Module: `engine/src/canvas-core/midCentury.ts`. Hero: a hillside village where a girl flies a kite.

**Medium, physically.** Opaque, matte designer's gouache on warm illustration board (Provensens, Mary Blair, Sasek). A flat brush lays each shape in, visible only at its slightly ragged edge; a nearly dry brush is dragged over the flats so the bristles skip the board's tooth; a loose brush-and-ink line goes on last, on its own plate, OFF REGISTER from the colour.

**The marks.** Flat opaque shapes whose edges are displaced geometry sized to the shape (polygons keep their corners: a house is cut, not rounded); dry-brush bristle streaks that break up as the brush runs out; a thin, slightly thick-thin line with open contours that wanders past the colour (plate offset +5, -4).

**Order.** Flat shapes BIG TO SMALL (sorted by area; the stacking order stays, so a house fills in around a hill already painted) -> dry brush (sky, clouds, hills, walls, dress shade, grass drifts) -> the line (the girl first, then kite, string, village, birds).

**Palette.** Warm board, mustard, tomato, turquoise, olive, deep green-black, pink, cream.

**Light.** Sun high left: darker right sides on every house and spire, the girl's flat shadow runs down the hill to the right.

**The human.** Eight years old, six heads (54 px head, 324 px figure): shoulders 1.3 heads, crotch 3.1, knees 4.5, ankles 5.8. Weight on the straight back leg; the front knee bends forward. Kite arm raised, elbow flexed outward-down, fist round the string with the thumb over it; balance arm back, hand open palm down, four fingers and a thumb. A-line dress, Peter Pan collar, puff sleeves, white socks, Mary Janes. Wind from the left: hem, bow tails, kite tail stream right.

**Not cut paper (fox).** Painted edges and dry brush instead of torn fibres and lifted pieces, and a line that floats off the colour.
