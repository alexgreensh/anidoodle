# Pencil & watercolour, drawn (`ranunculusDraw.ts`)

**Medium.** HB pencil and pan watercolour on cold-press, the hand of `ranunculus.ts`, whose
geometry, seeds and calls this film reuses verbatim (re-authored, so the hero stays byte-identical).

**Order of marks.** (1) Pencil lay-in, light enough to live under paint: bloom envelope, heart,
stem gesture, leaf axes, lips of the outer three rings. (2) First light washes, one per mass: the
bloom's wash follows the ring's scalloped silhouette (lip crowns walked round the centre, never a
disc), each wash spreads from where the brush touched along a ragged wet front and stops short
of its outline. (3) Form toward the one light (upper left): stem, leaves, calyx, then petals
outer ring to centre, each a wet front growing from the petal base. (4) Graphite over the paint:
lips and veins, stem and leaf contours; every line is ERASED where a petal painted later sits
over it (`destination-out` of the later petals' actual form outlines), because a hand does not
draw what it cannot see.

**Why the last frame is not the hero's pixels.** The hero interleaves paint and graphite petal by
petal and has no lay-in. Worked in the medium's order, the finish is the same flower with a
faint lay-in under the paint and all graphite on top.

**Motion grammar.** Pencil fast and light, washes slow (20 frames each), petals quicker as they
get smaller, graphite brisk. Wet fronts are sized to their shape so the front is still moving on
the op's last frame.
