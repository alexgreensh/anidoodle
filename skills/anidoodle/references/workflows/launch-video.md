# Launch and product videos

A launch video makes one promise about a product and shows it being kept. It is a short film
with a job: the viewer should know what the thing is, why it matters to them, and where to get
it, in that order.

## Inputs the person brings

Product screenshots, a logo, UI captures, a repo link, a feature list. Images they bring may be
embedded (pinned by sha256, provenance recorded in the film's manifest); everything around them
is drawn in code. Never generate or fetch images.

## Shape of the film

1. **Hook in the first two seconds**: the problem, drawn being made (a tangle, a pile, a clock),
   never a fade from black.
2. **The turn**: the product arrives. A screenshot slides in on a drawn device frame, or the
   UI is redrawn in the chosen hand.
3. **Three proofs at most**: each feature shown doing something, one per beat group, with a short
   drawn caption.
4. **The payoff and the call**: the product name drawn on, the one-line promise, where to get it.
   Hold it long enough to read twice.

Length follows the content: 15 s for a social teaser, 30-60 s for a launch post, longer for a
walkthrough (then use chapters, `long-film.md`). Ship the shapes where it will run: `9x16` for
Reels/Shorts, `16x9` for YouTube and the site, `1x1` for feeds.

## Craft notes

- **Kinetic type is lettering**, stroke-drawn in the chosen hand, timed to the beat grid.
- **Screenshots are content, not decoration**: large enough to read on a phone, framed, with the
  part that matters called out by a drawn circle or arrow in the style's hand.
- **Music**: a style that matches the brand (drive/electronic for energy, minimalist mallets for
  calm confidence, lo-fi for friendly), with the product reveal on a true sync point.
- **Brand colours** map onto the style's palette roles; the style's hand stays intact.
